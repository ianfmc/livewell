# Congressional Disclosure Data — Acquisition Method and Access Terms

Verified September 13, 2026. Scope: LIVEWELL, one individual’s private research for candidate NADEX trades; official House/Senate disclosures only. This is factual terms research, not legal advice.

## 1. Summary

Recommend **self-hosted Quantgress**, using its House and Senate PTR modules directly, with local DuckDB storage and optional local JSON access. The House provides annual ZIP filing indexes followed by individual PDF downloads; Senate automation reproduces the agreement-gated search interface and retrieves HTML reports through an undocumented portal endpoint. Both sources prohibit obtaining or using disclosures for commercial purposes, but the reviewed official pages do not expressly classify private research supporting personal trades. The acquisition route is technically concrete; automation authorization and that use classification remain unverified, rather than assumed permitted or prohibited. Use **jffrz78/congress-trades-app** as an OCR-capable fallback, with price enrichment disabled. [House overview](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure/ViewReport), [Senate agreement](https://efdsearch.senate.gov/search/home/), [Quantgress](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress), [fallback](https://github.com/jffrz78/congress-trades-app).

## 3. Access terms

**House reuse condition.** The search page prohibits “any commercial purpose, other than by news and communications media for dissemination to the general public”. It also bars unlawful purposes, credit-rating use, and solicitation of money. These restrictions concern information in disclosures, not merely republication. Private access, local storage, and absence of paying users therefore do not by themselves establish that every intended use is allowed. [House search notice](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure/ViewSearch).

**Senate reuse/access condition.** The agreement prohibits obtaining or using a report “for any commercial purpose, other than by news and communications media for dissemination to the general public”. It also includes the same unlawful-purpose, credit-rating and solicitation restrictions, and requires agreement before searching. No express personal-investment exception or definition of commercial purpose was found. **LIVEWELL’s specific classification is UNVERIFIED.** [Senate agreement](https://efdsearch.senate.gov/search/home/).

**Automation, both sources.** No explicit scraper prohibition, affirmative automation grant, or numeric official rate limit was located in the reviewed disclosure overview/search/agreement pages. The House’s linked [Privacy Policy](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/PrivacyPolicy) returned 404; neither host supplied a usable `robots.txt` (both returned 404). Senate HTML contains a `noindex` directive, which addresses search indexing rather than establishing scripted-download permission. Absence of a found restriction is not authorization. Quantgress imposes two-second delays itself; those are tool settings, not government rate limits. [House robots](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/robots.txt), [Senate robots](https://efdsearch.senate.gov/robots.txt), [House scraper](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/scrape_house.py), [Senate scraper](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/scrape_senate.py).

**Storage/retention.** No downloader-specific deletion deadline or local-cache prohibition was found in those portal pages. The statute’s retention section addresses reports retained by the filing agency/office, Clerk or Secretary and says “the report shall be destroyed unless needed in an ongoing investigation” after its applicable public-availability period. Members’ reports remain public until six years after leaving Congress; other reports generally have six-year availability, with specified unsuccessful-candidate exceptions. This text does **not expressly instruct recipients to delete downloaded copies**; that is a textual distinction, not a verified unlimited-retention entitlement. [5 U.S.C. § 13107(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A5+section%3A13107+edition%3Aprelim%29).

**Public records versus copyright versus access.** These are statutorily public disclosure records. The Copyright Office states “Copyright does not protect facts”; §105 excludes qualifying U.S. government works from copyright, with §101 defining those by preparation as part of official duties. That supports reuse of extracted factual fields as a copyright matter, but government hosting alone does not establish every candidate-authored report or third-party attachment as a government work. Blanket public-domain status for every document component is **UNVERIFIED**. Copyright status does not override the disclosure-use restrictions or establish automation permission. [Copyright FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-general.html), [17 U.S.C. §§101, 105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html), [official Senate public-access/use guidance](https://www.ethics.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/financialdisclosure).

**Recommendation rationale.** Quantgress best matches a single person’s local research workflow: it already combines both chambers, preserves source fields separately from inferred ticker values, and records processing status for resumable retrieval. Its license is concrete and its latest activity is recent. Use only the congressional modules and accept explicit coverage gaps rather than treating unparsed scans as no transactions. The fallback’s OCR is useful for those gaps, though its Markdown output is less convenient for automated ingestion and requires mapping into LIVEWELL’s existing schema. This recommendation selects software; it does not certify the unresolved official-use conditions.

**Recommended tool terms.** MIT grants rights “to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell”; its notice condition applies to “copies or substantial portions of the Software”. Preserve copyright and permission notices when copying the software; private execution is within the software grant. Neither the license nor README supplies a separate output-retention limit or a distinct normalized-data license overriding source restrictions. The README claims “Every other dataset (SEC EDGAR, USAspending, USPTO, LDA.gov, FAA, Senate/House disclosures, Wikimedia) is unrestricted public-domain U.S. government data.” Treat that as the maintainer’s assertion: it is broader than the official House/Senate notices support. [License](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/LICENSE), [README, Legal & Compliance](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/README.md#legal--compliance).

**Concrete implementation path.** Pin the reviewed revision; run the House and Senate `--selftest` checks offline, then bounded live runs only after settling the intended-use/access questions. Schedule `scrape_senate.py` and `scrape_house.py --year 2026` directly, retain their local database and processing statuses, and query the trades view or expose the API only locally. Do not use the stock `daily.py` for this scope: it also calls FINRA short-volume retrieval. `entities.py` can fetch SEC ticker data; omit it if strict House/Senate-only acquisition is required, retaining tickers already present in disclosure text. [PTR scripts](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/scrape_senate.py), [House script](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/scrape_house.py), [daily runner](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/daily.py), [entity resolver](https://github.com/DMulajkar/Quantgress/blob/main/entities.py).

## 5. Confirmed vs. unresolved

**Confirmed**

- House publishes annual filing indexes and individual PTR PDFs; the 2026 ZIP and a PTR PDF were successfully retrieved. Senate publishes agreement-gated disclosures including PTRs.
- Two recently active tools have actual MIT license files. Private use/copying of their software is permitted under that license, subject to its notice condition.
- Official prohibited-use conditions attach independently of code licensing and copyright. No reviewed page expressly gives personal trading research an exemption.
- No recipient-specific deletion deadline or official numeric request limit was located. This is a finding about reviewed sources, not a universal permission claim.
